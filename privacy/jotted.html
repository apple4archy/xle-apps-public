记了 Jotted 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

开发者：张琦

联系方式：通过 App Store 上的开发者联系方式 / App 产品页联系

「记了」（英文名 Jotted，以下称“本 App”）是一款在 iPhone 上记下任务和过程的工具。主屏显示名是「记了」。

我们重视你的隐私。简而言之：你的任务、过程记录和按日天气都只保存在这台 iPhone 上。本 App 不连接开发者的服务器，开发者看不到你的任何内容。本 App 没有账号，不追踪，没有广告，也没有第三方统计分析 SDK。

为了记下天气，每天第一次记录时，会把你的大致位置发送给 Apple 天气服务（WeatherKit）；地点名由系统的地理编码服务得出。这些请求发给 Apple，不是发给开发者。本 App 不保存你的经纬度。Apple 如何处理这些数据，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。你可以在设置里关闭天气；关闭后记录照常，只是不再获取天气。

1. 开发者不收集数据

  • 本 App 没有账号系统，不需要注册或登录。
  • 本 App 不包含任何统计分析、广告、崩溃上报或其他第三方 SDK。
  • 本 App 不连接开发者的服务器。开发者看不到你的任务、过程记录、天气或任何使用信息。
  • 本 App 不进行跟踪（tracking），也不使用广告标识符。
  • 为记下天气而向 Apple 发送的大致位置，由 Apple 按自己的隐私政策处理，开发者不会收到这些位置数据。

2. 你的数据保存在哪里

你在本 App 中产生的内容默认只保存在这台 iPhone 的本机存储中，包括：

  • 任务和过程记录；
  • 按日天气（含城市或区名，以及当时记下的天气信息）；
  • 本 App 的设置。

这些数据不会同步到开发者的服务器。本 App 自己不做云同步。删除本 App 会删除上述本机内容。

3. 天气与位置

开启天气后，本 App 在每天第一次主动记录时，通过 Apple 的 WeatherKit 获取当时当地的天气，一天一次。

  • 只在你使用本 App 时定位，且只用大致位置，不使用精确位置，也不在后台持续定位。
  • 本 App 不保存你的经纬度，只保存城市或区名，以及当时获取到的天气信息。
  • 地点名由系统的地理编码服务在设备上根据大致位置得出。
  • 导入备份不会触发天气获取。
  • 你可以随时在本 App 的设置里关闭天气。关闭后不再获取天气，也不再使用定位；已经记下的天气仍可查看，记录功能照常。
  • Apple 如何处理大致位置和天气请求，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

4. 语音识别

按住说话时，本 App 使用系统的语音识别，把你说的话转成文字并填入输入框。

  • 识别只在这台设备上进行。本 App 会把识别请求的 requiresOnDeviceRecognition 设为开启，并且只在设备端识别可用时才开始识别。
  • 本 App 不会把语音送到开发者的服务器，也不会在设备端不可用时改走云端识别。若这台设备当前无法使用设备端识别，本 App 会提示你改用键盘上的听写。
  • 若你改用系统键盘上的听写，听写由 iOS 提供，适用 Apple 的规则和隐私政策。

5. 搜索

搜索只在这台 iPhone 上进行，用于在当前列表里按关键字查找你的任务和过程记录。搜索不会把内容发送到开发者或任何服务器。

6. 本地提醒

你可以为某条任务设置一次提醒。提醒使用 iOS 的本地通知，在你选定的时间由这台设备发出，不经过任何服务器，也不经过开发者的服务器。

  • 每个任务最多一个提醒，再设置一次会覆盖原来的。
  • 第一次设置提醒时，本 App 才会申请通知权限；不设角标。
  • 你可以在 iPhone 的系统设置里关闭通知。
  • 提醒只记在系统的待触发通知里，不写入本 App 的数据库，也不会进入备份文件。从备份导入后，不会自动恢复提醒。完成、存档或删除任务时，该任务的提醒会被取消。

7. 导出与导入

「全部导出」会在你亲自操作时，生成一份未加密的备份文件，包含你的全部任务、过程记录和按日天气。这份文件由你自己保存和分享。开发者不会收到这份文件，除非你自己发送。

「从备份导入」只会和本机已有的内容合并，不会覆盖本机已有的任务、过程记录或天气。导入不会把你的内容发送到开发者的服务器。导入不会恢复本地提醒。

单条「复制过程记录」或系统分享，也只在你亲自操作时进行。

8. 权限说明

本 App 只在你使用相关功能时请求以下权限，你可以随时在 iPhone 的「设置」中更改：

  • 麦克风、语音识别： 用于按住说话，在本机把语音转成文字。识别只在设备上进行。
  • 定位（使用期间，大致位置）： 用于在每天第一次记录时记下当地天气。不允许定位时，记录照常，只是不记天气。
  • 通知： 用于在你设定的时间发出本地提醒。提醒不经过任何服务器。

拒绝某项权限只会影响对应的功能，不影响其他功能的使用。

9. 买断付费

本 App 在 App Store 以买断方式销售。费用由 Apple 通过 App Store 收取和处理。开发者拿不到你的支付卡号或其他支付信息。付款、发票和退款请通过 Apple 处理。

10. 数据删除

  • 删除本 App，会删除本机保存的全部任务、过程记录、按日天气和设置；尚未触发的本地提醒也会随之取消。
  • 你已经自行保存或分享出去的备份文件、过程记录文本，不会随 App 一起删除，需要你在「文件」或其他 App 里自行处理。
  • 本机彻底删除某条任务后，该任务不再出现在本 App 里；若它仍在你此前保存的备份中，之后「从备份导入」可能重新出现。

11. 儿童隐私

本 App 没有账号，开发者不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁（或你所在地区规定年龄）以下的儿童。若使用天气功能，大致位置会发送给 Apple 天气服务，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

12. 政策变更

如果本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。若未来版本开始由开发者收集任何数据，我们会在 App 内和本页面中明确说明，并在需要时征得你的同意。

13. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 App Store 上的开发者联系方式 / App 产品页联系。

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Developer: Zhang Qi

Contact: Please use the developer contact information on the App Store page or the App’s product page.

Jotted (记了, “the App”) is a tool for jotting down tasks and their progress on iPhone. The name shown on the Home Screen is「记了」.

We take your privacy seriously. In short: your tasks, process history, and daily weather stay on this iPhone. The App does not connect to any server operated by the developer, and the developer cannot see any of your content. The App has no accounts, does not track you, and includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

To save the weather, the first time you make a record each day, your approximate location is sent to the Apple Weather service (WeatherKit). The place name comes from the system geocoder. These requests go to Apple, not to the developer. The App does not store your coordinates. How Apple handles this data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. You can turn weather off in Settings; recording continues to work as usual, but the App will no longer fetch weather.

1. The developer collects no data

  • The App has no accounts; no sign-up or sign-in is required.
  • The App contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other third-party SDKs.
  • The App does not connect to any server operated by the developer. The developer cannot see your tasks, process history, weather, or any usage information.
  • The App does not track you and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • Approximate location sent to Apple for weather is handled under Apple’s privacy policy. The developer does not receive that location data.

2. Where your data is stored

What you create in the App is stored by default only in on-device storage on this iPhone, including:

  • tasks and process history;
  • daily weather (including the city or district name and the weather details saved at the time);
  • the App’s settings.

This data is not synced to any server operated by the developer. The App itself does not provide cloud sync. Deleting the App deletes this on-device data.

3. Weather and location

When weather is turned on, the App uses Apple WeatherKit to fetch the local weather at the moment of your first active record of the day, once per day.

  • Location is used only while you are using the App, and only at reduced (approximate) accuracy. The App does not use precise location and does not track you in the background.
  • The App does not store your coordinates. It stores only a city or district name, plus the weather details fetched at that time.
  • The place name is obtained from the system geocoder using your approximate location.
  • Importing a backup does not trigger a weather fetch.
  • You can turn weather off at any time in the App’s Settings. After that, the App will not fetch weather or use location. Weather already saved remains visible, and recording continues as usual.
  • How Apple handles approximate location and weather requests is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Speech recognition

When you hold to talk, the App uses system speech recognition to turn what you say into text and place it in the input field.

  • Recognition runs on this device only. The App sets requiresOnDeviceRecognition on the recognition request and starts recognition only when on-device recognition is available.
  • The App does not send your speech to the developer’s servers, and it will not fall back to cloud recognition if on-device recognition is unavailable. In that case, the App asks you to use dictation on the system keyboard instead.
  • If you use dictation on the system keyboard, that feature is provided by iOS and is subject to Apple’s rules and privacy policy.

5. Search

Search runs only on this iPhone. It lets you find your tasks and process history by keyword in the current list. Search does not send your content to the developer or to any server.

6. Local reminders

You can set a one-time reminder on a task. Reminders use iOS local notifications. They fire on this device at the time you choose, and they do not pass through any server, including any server operated by the developer.

  • Each task can have at most one reminder; setting a new one replaces the previous one.
  • The App asks for notification permission only the first time you set a reminder. It does not set a badge.
  • You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings.
  • Reminders live only in the system’s pending notifications. They are not stored in the App’s database and are not included in backup files. Importing a backup does not restore reminders. Completing, archiving, or deleting a task cancels its reminder.

7. Export and import

“Export All” creates an unencrypted backup file when you choose to do so. The file contains all of your tasks, process history, and daily weather. You keep and share this file yourself. The developer will not receive it unless you send it.

“Import from Backup” merges with what is already on this device. It does not overwrite tasks, process history, or weather already stored here. Import does not send your content to any server operated by the developer. Import does not restore local reminders.

Copying a single task’s process history, or using the system share sheet, also happens only when you start it.

8. Permissions

The App asks for these permissions only when you use the related feature. You can change them at any time in iOS Settings:

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: to turn your speech into text on this device when you hold to talk. Recognition runs on-device only.
  • Location (When In Use, approximate): to save the local weather the first time you make a record each day. If you decline location access, recording continues as usual; only weather is skipped.
  • Notifications: to fire a local reminder at the time you set. Reminders do not pass through any server.

Declining a permission only disables the related feature.

9. Paid purchase

The App is sold as a one-time purchase on the App Store. Apple collects and processes the payment through the App Store. The developer does not receive your card number or other payment details. For billing, invoices, or refunds, please go through Apple.

10. Deleting your data

  • Deleting the App removes all tasks, process history, daily weather, and settings stored on this device. Any pending local reminders are cancelled as well.
  • Backup files or process-history text that you have already saved or shared are not removed with the App; you can delete those yourself in Files or in the other app you used.
  • If you permanently delete a task in the App, it disappears from the App. If that task is still in a backup file you kept, “Import from Backup” may bring it back.

11. Children’s privacy

The App has no accounts, and the developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age required in your region). If weather is used, approximate location is sent to the Apple Weather service and is subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version ever has the developer collect any data, we will say so clearly in the App and on this page, and ask for your consent where required.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the developer contact information on the App Store page or the App’s product page.
